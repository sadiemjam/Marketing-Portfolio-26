About Me

Hi, I'm Sadie!

I'm a Commerce student at Queen's University with a passion for marketing, branding, and creating content that connects with people. I'm especially interested in combining creativity with strategy, understanding what an audience wants and turning those insights into content and ideas that resonate.

Through my marketing and internship experiences, I've worked on social media strategy, content creation, competitive research, brand positioning, and audience targeting. I love taking an idea from the brainstorming stage and turning it into something people can see, interact with, and remember.

Outside of marketing, I enjoy getting involved in my community, meeting new people, travelling, and trying new things. I bring curiosity, creativity, and a collaborative mindset to everything I do, and I'm always looking for opportunities to learn, create, and make an impact.
